Request deletion of your data

How to ask us to delete your Wigan Pool League account and the personal data behind it.

Send a deletion request

Email us from the address registered to your account and tell us you'd like your account and data deleted.

wiganpool@hotmail.com

Include the name and email address on your account so we can find it.

We aim to complete deletions within 30 days of a verified request. We'll confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

DataOutcome
Player profile — name, email, phone numberDeleted
Login credentialsDeleted
Push notification tokenDeleted
Match results, frame scores & league standings you're part ofRetained

Match and standings records are kept in anonymised or aggregate form, as they form part of the shared league record for teams and competitions you played in. No personal profile data remains attached to them.

Partial deletion

If you'd rather keep playing but want specific data removed — for example your phone number, or push notifications turned off — say so in your email and we'll action just that instead of closing the account.
