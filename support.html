App support

Help with the Wigan Pool League app — fixtures, results, standings and match-night scorecards for the league.

Contact us

Having a problem, spotted a wrong result, or have a suggestion? Email the league and we'll get back to you.

wiganpool@hotmail.com

It helps to include your name, team, the device you're using (iPhone / Android) and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Common questions

I can't log in or have forgotten my password

Check you're using the email address you registered with. If you still can't get in, email us from that address and we'll help you regain access.

A match result or score is wrong

Results are entered by team captains and the league committee. Speak to your captain first; if it still needs correcting, email us with the fixture date, the teams involved and what should be changed.

I'm not on my team's player list

Team captains manage their squad entries. Ask your captain to add you, or contact the league if your captain can't.

I'm not getting notifications

Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings, and check your notification preferences on the Profile screen inside the app.

The app isn't showing the latest fixtures or results

Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen the app. If it still looks out of date, make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
